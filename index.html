About me

Good product decisions start with understanding how people actually behave.

My M.Sc. in Economics at Heidelberg University focused on behavioural economics: applied econometrics, advanced statistical analysis and modelling behavioural data. Before that I spent two years at Yektanet, a digital advertising platform with over 60 million daily users, first as a working student in product analytics and then as a product management intern.

As an intern I worked on publisher recommendations and a bot that automates ad submission: talking to users, turning their needs into user stories, prioritising the backlog with the product lead and testing every release. My analytics background helps me check whether a change actually worked. On the side, I build small products of my own to practise the full cycle, from discovery to a working MVP.
