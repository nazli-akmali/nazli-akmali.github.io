About me

Numbers are most useful when they explain how people behave.

My M.Sc. in Economics at Heidelberg University focused on behavioural economics: applied econometrics, advanced statistical analysis and modelling behavioural data. Before that I spent two years at Yektanet, a digital advertising platform with over 60 million daily users, first as a working student in product analytics and then as a product management intern focused on data analysis.

There I learned to work close to the product: finding where things break, building the dashboards teams check every day, and turning analysis into concrete backlog items. On the side, I build small apps of my own to practise the full product cycle, from discovery to a working MVP.
